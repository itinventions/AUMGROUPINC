ARTISANAL GOLDSMITHING

Forged with Generational Weight.

At AUM GROUP INC., gold is not treated as a mere setting for stones—it is celebrated as an expressive sculpture. We forge exclusively in solid 18K gold (750‰ purity), balanced with copper and silver to produce our signature warm straw yellow, velvet rose, and crisp white alloys.

Every bangle hinge is calibrated by hand with internal tension springs; every collar link is hand-knitted to drape like liquid satin across the collarbone.
