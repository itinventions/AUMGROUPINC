INDEPENDENT LABORATORY CERTIFICATION

GIA & IGI Certified Diamond Assurance

At Aum Group Inc., every loose diamond can be supplied with independent third-party grading reports. Clients have the complete flexibility to select GIA or IGI certification upon request, ensuring absolute peace of mind and global retail verification.

Global Benchmark

GIA Certified Diamonds

Gemological Institute of America

The creator of the universal 4Cs (Cut, Clarity, Color, Carat Weight) and the world's most trusted benchmark in natural gemstone analysis. Every GIA dossier features microscopic laser inscription on the diamond girdle, comprehensive proportion diagrams, and conflict-free origin verification.

Universal 4Cs Standard Laser Inscription Digital Verification Conflict-Free Origin
International Laboratory

IGI Certified Diamonds

International Gemological Institute

The world's largest independent gemological testing laboratory, celebrated by jewelry manufacturers across Europe, North America, and Asia. Globally renowned for precision cut analysis, optical symmetry evaluation, and tamper-proof sealed security packaging for loose stones and calibrated melee parcels.

Cut Precision Grading Optical Symmetry Color Authenticity Sealed Security Parcels

100% Natural Earth-Mined

Guaranteed authentic natural diamonds, rigorously tested to ensure zero synthetic mixing.

Kimberley Process Compliant

Full compliance with United Nations resolutions, assuring 100% ethical, conflict-free sourcing.
