Our Four Pillars of Speciality

Setting international benchmarks for rarity, gemological integrity, bespoke artistry, and client security.

Certified Diamond Vault

Every natural and lab-grown diamond is individually graded and verified by GIA or IGI, guaranteeing exact cut precision, color purity, and conflict-free provenance.

Rare Untreated Gemstones

Direct mine-to-market access to unheated Burma rubies, Ceylon sapphires, and Muzo emeralds, each accompanied by premier SSEF or Gübelin gemological certificates.

Master Craftsmanship Since 2011

Over a decade of haute joaillerie experience. Every mounting is custom cast, hand-assembled, and microscope-set by master artisans in our dedicated ateliers.

Global Armored Delivery

Discreet, fully insured worldwide transit managed through premier secure couriers (Malca-Amit & Brink's), delivering with end-to-end provenance to over 40 countries.

OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

Where Rare Earth Minerals Become Living Heirlooms

Founded in 2011, AUM GROUP INC. began with an uncompromising mission: to connect discerning private collectors and fine jewellery connoisseurs with Earth’s rarest mineral treasures.

From our origins in the historic gemstone corridors of Mumbai, we have expanded into an internationally respected fine jewellery house known for certified diamond integrity, untreated coloured gemstones, and sculptural precious metal artistry.

Every creation is born from a dialogue between raw nature and human mastery. Our lapidaries respect each crystal’s unique optical axis, revealing its deepest fire and enduring brilliance across generations.
