Our Craftsmanship Story

From Ethical Mine to Living Heirloom

Every creation is born from a rigorous four-stage journey of ethical sourcing, master faceting, and bench goldsmithing.

01 • Direct Sourcing 💎

Ethical Mine-to-Market

Direct sourcing of conflict-free rough diamonds and untreated colored gemstones straight from certified mines, eliminating trading markups.

02 • Precision Cut ✨

57/58-Facet Symmetry

Our generational master polishers in Mumbai apply exact mathematical angles to unleash maximum optical fire, brilliance, and scintillation.

03 • Bench Artistry 🔥

Master Goldsmithing

Solid 18K/22K gold casting, micro-pavé hand setting under high magnification, and Italian Florentine hand-engraved texturing.

04 • Provenance 🛡️

GIA / IGI Certification

Each jewel receives individual laboratory certification with laser inscribed provenance, sealed for armored global delivery to 40+ nations.
