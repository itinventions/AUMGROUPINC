OUR HERITAGE • PRIVATE ATELIER

Where Artistry Meets Eternity

AUM GROUP INC. was founded on an enduring reverence for precious gemstone lapidary and fine goldsmithing. What began as a dedicated atelier has grown into an international house trusted by discerning collectors and private clients worldwide.

Every piece is shaped by hand in limited batches—forged from recycled 18K solid gold, hypoallergenic Argentium 925 silver, and natural untreated gems curated from ethical mines across Colombia, Kashmir, and Burma.
